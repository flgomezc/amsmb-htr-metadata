PageXML Annotation Viewer

Drop one charter's JPEG and its eScriptorium PageXML. The page draws every line and zone over the image, converts them to W3C Web Annotations, and can render those annotations again with Annotorious, a standard W3C viewer. Your files stay in your browser and are not uploaded.

Line: annotation with motivation "describing" Zone: annotation with motivation "classifying" Hovered or selected Drawn by Annotorious from the generated W3C JSON-LD. Baselines are not drawn because W3C annotations have no baseline concept; each annotation links to its PageXML line instead. Shapes can be selected but not edited.

    W3C Web Annotations

    One AnnotationPage per charter. Each line is an Annotation whose body is the transcription and whose target is the polygon on the image (SvgSelector, plus a bounding-box FragmentSelector). Zones become classifying annotations tagged with their SegmOnto type. Every annotation also has a linking body with an XPathSelector that points to its TextLine or TextRegion in the PageXML, so the baseline and other line metadata are not lost. When the file name matches one of the 100 charters, the target is the JPEG's Dataverse link from amsmb_charters_instances.jsonld.
